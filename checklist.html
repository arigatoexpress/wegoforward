The 10-minute small-business security checklist

Answer these ten questions honestly. Every "no" or "not sure" is a fix worth doing this month. Based on free guidance from CISA and NIST.

  1. Two-step sign-in: is it turned on for your business email, bank, payroll, payment processor and website admin?
  2. Admins: can you list everyone who has admin access to those accounts, and has anyone left the business since?
  3. Passwords: does your team use a password manager instead of a notebook or a shared sheet?
  4. Backups: are your important files and email backed up somewhere separate, and have you ever actually restored one?
  5. Updates: do your computers and phones install updates automatically?
  6. Customer data: do you know where you keep customers' Social Security, license or bank numbers, and who can open them?
  7. Old records: do you have a written rule for shredding or deleting old customer records? (Colorado law expects one.)
  8. Scams: does your team know to call back on a known number before changing payment details or sending money?
  9. AI tools: do you know which AI tools your staff use and what customer information goes into them?
  10. Bad day: if your email were taken over tonight, do you know the first three calls you would make?

Three or more "no" answers? That is a common result for this list. The checklist does not fix them. If you want to talk about the website assistant, book a call. A written quote names the scope before any work.
