WeGoForward. Not Texas Home Outlet.

One workflow, one week

For one owner. One job. Done when a person can run it without you.

You leave with

One of these, picked before the week starts:

  • Drafts of replies, sitting unsent, for a person to send.
  • A list of rows that miss one field you named. Nobody is contacted.

Plus a one-page receipt: what it read, what it wrote, and what it is forbidden to do.

You do not get

A second workflow, a login to your bank, texts to customers, or a claim that leads went up.

Shape

Five days. Day one is the scope, in writing. Day five is you running it once while someone watches.

Price

Not set. Advertised one-automation projects run about $5,000–$30,000. Only a small fixed job fits here. The number is a separate yes.
