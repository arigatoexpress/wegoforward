Half day: build an agent that stops

For a team that wants to use AI on real work and is about to let it send, spend, or post.

You leave with

  • One job written down: what the agent reads, what it drafts, and the line it may not cross.
  • A working exercise: workshop/exercise.cjs. An inbox note goes in, an unsent draft comes out. A phone number that was not allowed does not appear in the draft. Check it with node --test workshop/exercise.test.cjs.
  • The one security rule: if it can send, it is not done.
  • A list of what your team will not automate.

You do not get

A chatbot for your website, a stack of tools, or a promise of hours saved.

Shape

About four hours. You bring one real task with the names removed. The room does the exercise on that task.
