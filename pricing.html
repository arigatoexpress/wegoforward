Common questions

Does the assistant send messages?

No. It may draft. A person sends. It does not book, text, or spend on its own.

What if it does not know the answer?

The question goes to a person. It answers the questions you approved.

How do payments work?

Payment terms are set out in your written quote before work starts.

What is not promised?

The assistant does not promise more leads, more sales, a reply time, or that the site stays up. Demos on this site are fictional examples.
