Approach

Careful on purpose.

The point is not to automate the most. It is to hand over work that stays handed over.

  • If it can send, it is not done.

    Every build stops at a draft. A person presses send, spends, or posts — never the agent on its own.

  • Scope in writing, first.

    Day one is the scope, in writing. No scope, no build. If it takes more than one sentence, it is two jobs.

  • A one-page receipt.

    What it read, what it wrote, and what it is forbidden to do. You keep the page.

  • Prices are approved before they appear.

    Market ranges are labeled as other people's ads, not this invoice. A price shows up only once it is approved.
