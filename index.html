Fit

Who it's for

Independent repair shops. Not a phone service, and not a shop that wants the assistant to send on its own.

  • A shop that hears the same questions

    Hours, what you fix, and whether you are taking work. About 15 answers you approve. Anything else goes to a person.

Limits

What this assistant does not do

  • It does not send.

    A person says yes before anything is sent, booked, posted, or deleted.

  • It does not answer the phone.

    Texting, a calendar, and hosting are not part of these two tiers.

  • It does not guess.

    A question you have not approved goes to a person. No count of leads, reply time, or promise that the site stays up is included.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Does the assistant send messages?

No. It may draft. A person sends. It does not book, text, or spend on its own.

What if it does not know the answer?

The question goes to a person. It answers only the questions you approved.

What does it cost?

Website answers is $495 setup + $99/month. Lead follow-up is $995 setup + $249/month. These are starting points. A written quote names the scope before any work.

Are the demos real shops?
