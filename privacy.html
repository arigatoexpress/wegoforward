Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-04. Short, because there is not much to say.

This website

  • The contact page has one form: name, shop, email, and a question. A hidden field catches simple bots. Cloudflare Turnstile checks the submission before it is emailed to me. I use that note only to reply and to do work you ask for. I do not sell it.
  • This site sets no cookies and has no advertising trackers. Besides the spam check on the contact page, the only outside script is Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookie-free visit counter added by the host.
  • On each page view, the counter sends Cloudflare the page address, the referring page, your browser type (user agent) and page load timings. It stores nothing on your device.
  • If you pick a light or dark theme, your browser remembers that choice on your device. Nothing is sent. /assets/app.js saves it in the browser's local storage, and it stays on the device.
  • It is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes standard request data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and protect the site. Cloudflare also produces aggregate, cookie-free visit statistics for this site. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for details.
  • If you contact me through the form or through LinkedIn, I use what you send only to reply to you and to deliver work you ask for. I do not sell or share it. The spam check loads a script from Cloudflare at challenges.cloudflare.com. With JavaScript off, use LinkedIn.

Client projects

  • Your records and leads stay in your accounts. I work through admin access you grant and can remove.
  • If your project includes an AI helper, the questions people type into it are sent to the AI provider named in your quote and processed under that provider's terms.
  • I do not keep copies of your customer data on my own accounts or devices. Notes and reports contain only what is needed to do the work.
  • I remove my access when a project ends and give you a list of what I changed.
  • Demos and tests use made-up data, never real customer records.

Your choices

You can ask what information I hold about you, and ask me to correct or delete it. Use the contact options on the contact page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
