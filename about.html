About

Hi, I'm Ari

I build one website assistant for independent repair shops. It answers the questions you approve, and it drafts follow-up for a person to send.

I build working systems and pay attention to security from the start. The service for sale on this site is one website assistant for an independent repair shop. It is set up to stop and ask before it sends or spends.

I practice the pieces a shop system can include: a website, a catalog, a place for enquiries, and an assistant that drafts for a person. The case study on this site is an example scenario, not a client result. I explain the work in plain words.

By day I work full time as an operations supervisor in logistics, so I know what it is like to run a shift where things have to work the first time. WeGoForward work happens on a fixed schedule, and I only take on what I can finish well.

I live in the Gunnison Valley and love what this place is about: small businesses, neighbors who know each other, and the outdoors. I can meet a shop here in person, and I can work with a shop remotely.

What I believe

  • If I do not know, I say so.
