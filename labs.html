Agents and Crypto Lab

Where I build and test tools for AI agents and on-chain systems, in the open. The Lab sharpens the same safety skills I bring to small businesses.

Agent spend guard

An open-source guard that lets an AI agent do useful work but stops it before it pays, sends or signs past limits you set. Every decision leaves a receipt a person can check. In development, test networks only.

Agent tooling

Model Context Protocol (MCP) tools that connect agents to real systems with tight permissions and tests.

Agent payments research

Notes on how agents pay for things (such as the x402 standard) and where the security risks are. Research only, on test networks.

Small and honest

The Lab is a small, part-time effort. It ships little, carefully, and says plainly what is unfinished.

Lab rules

  • No tokens. No client money or keys are ever held. Test networks only.
